# QuikCad Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how QuikCad ("QuikCad," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the QuikCad application.

By using QuikCad, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

QuikCad collects certain information necessary to provide personal accounts, business accounts, subscriptions, project features, and account synchronization.

### Account Information

When you create or use a QuikCad account, we may collect and store information including:

- Email address
- Account identifier
- Firebase User ID (UID)
- Account type
- Account status
- Account creation date
- Subscription type and subscription status

Authentication credentials are processed using Google Firebase Authentication.

QuikCad does not have access to your plaintext password.

### Business Account Information

If you create, own, or join a QuikCad Business Account, we may collect and store information including:

- Business name
- Business identifier
- Company code
- Business owner email address
- Business owner Firebase UID
- Business membership information
- Number of business members
- Business seat limit
- Business subscription type
- Business account status
- Business account creation date

Company codes are used to allow authorized users to join a QuikCad Business Account.

### Project and Collaboration Information

When using QuikCad project synchronization or Business Account collaboration features, information associated with projects may be stored or associated with your account or business.

This may include information necessary to identify project ownership, business membership, project access, and sharing permissions.

### Technical Information

QuikCad or services used by QuikCad may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the app, such as:

- Device type
- Operating system version
- App version
- Crash information
- Performance information
- Authentication and security information

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage QuikCad accounts
- Authenticate users
- Provide access to personal and business accounts
- Allow users to join businesses using company codes
- Manage Business Account membership and seat limits
- Provide project synchronization and sharing features
- Determine account and subscription access
- Maintain the security of QuikCad
- Provide customer support
- Diagnose technical problems
- Improve the reliability and performance of the app

We do not sell your personal information.

---

## 3. Business Accounts

QuikCad allows businesses to create Business Accounts and allow additional users to join those accounts.

Users who join a Business Account may have access to projects or information shared within that business.

Certain account information may be associated with the Business Account in order to manage membership, permissions, subscription limits, and project access.

Business owners or administrators may be able to manage users associated with their Business Account.

Users should only share a Company Code with people they authorize to join their business.

---

## 4. Firebase and Google Services

QuikCad uses Google Firebase services to provide portions of its account and cloud functionality.

These services may include:

### Firebase Authentication

Used to:

- Create accounts
- Sign users in
- Authenticate users
- Associate users with QuikCad account information

### Cloud Firestore

Used to securely store information associated with QuikCad accounts and Business Accounts.

Information stored in Firestore may include account identifiers, email addresses, business information, company codes, subscription information, membership information, and other information necessary to operate QuikCad.

Google may process information necessary to provide Firebase services in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and data-processing terms.

For more information about Google and Firebase privacy practices, visit Google's Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

---

## 5. Subscriptions and Payments

QuikCad subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store are processed by Apple.

QuikCad does not directly collect or store your:

- Credit card number
- Debit card number
- Apple payment credentials
- Full payment card information

We may receive information from Apple necessary to determine subscription status, product entitlement, or access to paid QuikCad features.

Apple processes payments according to Apple's own privacy policy and terms.

---

## 6. Free Trials

QuikCad may offer free trials for certain subscription products.

When a free trial expires, continued access to paid features may require an active subscription.

Subscription management, cancellation, and billing are handled through Apple for App Store purchases.

---

## 7. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information associated with QuikCad accounts.

Account and Business Account information may be stored using Google Firebase services.

While we take reasonable precautions to protect user information, no electronic storage, database, or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and should not share passwords with other people.

---

## 8. Company Codes

Business Accounts may receive a unique Company Code.

Company Codes are intended to be shared only with people authorized to join that Business Account.

Anyone who obtains a valid Company Code may potentially attempt to join the associated business, subject to QuikCad's account and membership restrictions.

Business owners are responsible for controlling who receives their Company Code.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain account and Business Account information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Maintain your QuikCad account
- Provide QuikCad services
- Maintain Business Account membership
- Provide subscription access
- Resolve technical or security issues
- Meet legal or regulatory requirements

Information that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

## 11. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your QuikCad account and personal information.

To request account or personal data deletion, contact:

**support@barrasoft.com**

When an account deletion request is completed, personal information associated with the account will be deleted where reasonably possible, except where information must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, billing, or regulatory purposes.

If you belong to a Business Account, certain business information that belongs to the business rather than specifically to your personal account may remain after your account is removed.

Deleting your QuikCad account does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription.

Subscriptions purchased through Apple must be cancelled through your Apple account or App Store subscription settings.
